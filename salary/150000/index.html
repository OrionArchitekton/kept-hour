$150,000 a year is not $72.12 an hour.

Dividing by 2,080 assumes a 40-hour week and a 52-week year. At 45 hours and 50 weeks, $150,000 is $66.67 before tax. After a 25% planning load, the kept hour is $50.00. Change the assumptions. The page will not argue with you.

What to do with $150,000

If an offer matches this salary and adds hours, the kept hour is the comparison, not the title. A $150,000 job at 55 hours pays less per kept hour than a lower salary at 40, once tax is the same. Run both offers in the calculator and keep the higher hour.

Evenings already sold are the hours above 40, times weeks worked. They are not a moral score. They are the number you can put next to a hobby, a child, or a second skill that might raise the next salary without raising the week.
